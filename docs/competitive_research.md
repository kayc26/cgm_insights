## 2. Proposed softened claim

> Most apps in this category score or summarize a meal after it's eaten; a few — Levels' meal comparison, January AI's food-swap predictions, Signos' personalized food rankings — go further and offer some form of forward-looking or swap-style guidance. None of them, as far as I can verify, fit a model to one person's own glucose-response data and simulate a specific counterfactual change to a specific meal with a predicted mg/dL effect — that narrower combination is the gap this project fills.

## Competitive landscape

Most CGM apps in this category summarize or score a meal after it's eaten. A smaller set goes further and offers some kind of forward-looking or swap-style guidance:

| Product | What it offers | Gap vs. this project |
|---|---|---|
| Levels | Compares two already-logged meals side by side; generic swap tips (protein/fat/fiber first) | Retrospective comparison, not a simulated counterfactual on an upcoming meal; tips aren't fit to a personal model |
| January AI | Predicts a food's glucose impact pre-meal, personalizes after ~5 days of CGM + food logging; suggests food swaps | Personalization method is unpublished — likely cohort-calibrated rather than a from-scratch fit on one person's data |
| Signos | Learns which foods spike a given user; recommends what to eat next | Ranks food choices going forward, not a modeled delta for changing the specific meal in front of the user |
| Veri | Scores logged meals from glucose response + a population "Food Quality" label | Its own copy calls the dietary guidance "generalized"; no per-meal simulated swap with a predicted number |
| Abbott Lingo | Weekly reports built on five general behavioral principles | Retrospective and reactive by design; no pre-meal, per-meal simulation |

The gap this project targets is narrow: a model fit to one person's own glucose-response data, simulating a specific counterfactual edit to a specific meal, reporting a predicted mg/dL effect — rather than a score, a population-derived rating, or a general tip.

*Capabilities surveyed July 2026; these products ship updates frequently and may have changed since.*
